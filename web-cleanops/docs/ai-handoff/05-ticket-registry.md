## Status values
- DRAFT: ticket exists but is not ready for RORK execution.
- READY: ticket is approved for RORK execution.
- IN_PROGRESS: RORK is actively working on the ticket.
- DONE: ticket has been implemented and verified.
- BLOCKED: ticket cannot continue under the escalation policy.
- SKIPPED: ticket was intentionally not executed.
